# CLAUDE.md — PFE: Personalized Tourist Itinerary Recommendation

Master's thesis: **"Smart Visit Module for Personalized Tourist Itinerary Recommendation
Based on User Preferences and Contextual Data."** A personalized, context-aware next-POI
**engine**, decoded into tourist **itineraries**, and benchmarked against the literature.

## The two tasks (NEVER cross-compare their metrics)
- **Phase 1 — next-POI**: predict the single next place. Foursquare NYC, full-vocab **HR@k / NDCG@k / MRR**.
- **Phase 2 — itinerary**: produce the whole **ordered route**. Metrics **F1 + pairs-F1** (Chen 2016).
- Two distinct sub-tasks, each on its own field-standard benchmark. Comparing a next-POI HR@1 to an itinerary pairs-F1 (or scores across datasets with different POI-vocab sizes) is invalid. See `evaluation_methodology.md`.

## Methods — internal letters → reader-facing names
The internal design IDs **"Strategy A/B/C/D"** are renamed in **reader-facing materials only**
(presentation + report). **CODE keeps the letters/symbols** (`rollout_greedy`, `PointerItineraryModel`,
`src/itinerary/`, etc.) and `flickr_results_data.py` keeps its `AVSB_NYC` keys.
- **Strategy A → "Frozen Rollout"** — decode the *frozen* next-POI engine (no extra training). **The headline method.**
- **Strategy B → "Trained Pointer"** — GCN+pointer trained end-to-end (B-v1 no context, B-v2 +context). **Did NOT beat A.**
- **Strategy C** — orienteering-problem / ILS reference: **never implemented**. Do NOT present it as a method/result; at most one future-work line ("scoped, not implemented").
- **Strategy D → "Flickr Benchmark"** — re-run on the Flickr datasets under the Chen-2016 protocol; the **literature-comparable validation** (the only valid bridge to other papers' numbers).

## Key results
- **Engine (Phase 1, NYC):** HR@1 = 0.187 — honest LSTM/STGCN tier (below transformer/LLM SOTA).
- **Itinerary NYC (len≥3):** Frozen Rollout ≈ 0.29 pairs-F1 > Trained Pointer ≈ 0.26 (integration did NOT win — a supervision-density effect; nuances Halder/DLIR).
- **Flickr Benchmark:** **Random reproduces Chen 2016** (validates the harness); our classical 0.23–0.59, pointer 0.31–0.49 — on the published **0.26–0.85** scale. Published SOTA: SelfTrip/AR-Trip ≈ 0.80.
- **Personalization** (user-embedding ablation, Flickr): mixed — Glasgow +0.038, Osaka/Toronto ~neutral (cold-start: few trips per user under leave-one-out).

## Comparability rule (load-bearing)
Compare numbers ONLY within identical **dataset + protocol + metric**. NYC itinerary numbers are
NOT comparable to the Flickr literature — that's exactly why the Flickr Benchmark exists. Protocol for
the itinerary task: leave-one-trajectory-out CV, first+last POI given, length≥3, loop-free, pairs-F1.
